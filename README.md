# Retail Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Dashboard

![Dashboard preview](dashboard.png.png)

**Personal project** · Python · SQL · Power BI · DAX

## Problem statement
Retail teams need a clear view of revenue, profitability, customer behaviour and regional trends over several years. This project analyses multi-year sales data to answer those questions and tracks the key measures in one Power BI dashboard.

## Dataset
Multi-year retail sales data. The dashboard shown here is filtered to 1 January – 31 December 2016.

## Project steps
- Cleaned the data with Pandas.
- Wrote SQL queries for revenue, profit margin, order volume and discount impact.
- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with executive KPIs.
- Wrote DAX measures for YoY growth, average order value and profit margin.

## Key insights
- Total revenue for 2016 is $609.2K and total profit is $81.8K, a 13.43% profit margin on 9,837 units.
- Revenue grew 29.5% year on year, with month-on-month growth of 3.8%.
- Technology brings in the most revenue ($226.4K, 37.16%) and the most profit ($39.8K) from the fewest units (1,698).
- Furniture earns $198.9K in revenue but only $7.0K in profit, a 3.50% margin, against 19.06% for Office Supplies and 17.57% for Technology.
- Revenue is strongest from September onward and peaks in December at close to $100K.
- The top product, a Canon imageCLASS model, earns about $26K, roughly double the next best (about $13K).

## Dashboard
Growth KPIs, monthly revenue and profit, top 10 products, category profit margins and revenue share, with category, subcategory and date filters.

## Business impact
Potential impact: gives decision-makers a single view of revenue, profit margin, order volume and discount impact, with year-on-year growth and average order value tracked through DAX measures.
